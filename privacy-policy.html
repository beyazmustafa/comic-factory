Comic Factory Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Comic Factory is a private content automation application used to create and publish comic-book related short-form videos to authorized social media accounts.

This Privacy Policy explains how Comic Factory handles information when interacting with Meta and Instagram APIs.

2. Information We Access

Comic Factory may access limited information required by Instagram APIs in order to operate the application.

This may include:

  • Instagram account identifiers
  • Account authorization tokens
  • Media publishing permissions
  • Information required to publish authorized media

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Meta or Instagram APIs is used only to provide the application's intended functionality.

This includes:

  • Authenticating an authorized Instagram account
  • Publishing video content to that account
  • Managing media created by Comic Factory
  • Performing technical operations required for publishing

4. Data Sharing and Selling

Comic Factory does not sell personal information.

Information obtained through Meta or Instagram APIs is not sold, rented, or provided to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

5. Data Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens required for application operation are stored using secured environment secrets and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Temporary media files may be transferred to a hosting provider when required for Instagram media publishing. Temporary hosted copies may be removed after the publishing process completes.

6. Third-Party Services

Comic Factory may interact with third-party services necessary for its operation, including:

  • Meta and Instagram APIs
  • GitHub Actions
  • Cloudinary
  • Google services
  • Other technical services required for media processing

These services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention

Comic Factory retains only information reasonably necessary to operate the application, prevent duplicate publishing, and maintain authorized account connections.

Temporary data may be deleted when it is no longer required.

8. User Data Deletion

Users may revoke Comic Factory's access to their Instagram account through their Meta or Instagram account settings.

A user may also request deletion of information associated with Comic Factory by contacting the application owner through the Instagram account connected to the application.

Upon a valid deletion request, stored application data associated with that account will be deleted when technically and legally appropriate.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and application data.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Comic Factory is not designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change.

The latest version will remain available at this URL.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data deletion, contact the owner through the Instagram account connected to Comic Factory.
